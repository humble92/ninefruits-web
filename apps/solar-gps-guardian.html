Solar GPS Guardian

A public dashboard and email alert for GPS risk from space weather. Open the page, check a place or a drive, and subscribe with an email and a region. There is no account.

Purpose

Make a solar storm visible as a GPS risk, for a location and for a route, before it is only a headline. The score is a fixed formula over public feeds. A language model may rephrase the sentence only when it repeats that score, and it drafts a monthly newsletter from archived measurements.

Technology stack

Next.js 15 React 19 TypeScript Leaflet Python FastAPI Modal SQLite Baseten Fireworks

The page is a Next.js app. Python on Modal ingests open data every five minutes, scores risk, and sends mail. SQLite on a Modal volume stores the latest snapshot and subscriptions. Map tiles are OpenStreetMap via Leaflet. Place lookup and driving geometry use public Nominatim and OSRM. Baseten, then Fireworks, are optional writers. If they are absent, or if they change the score, the template sentence stays.

Features

satellite_alt

Live conditions

Kp, the three-day forecast, the strongest recent flare, local conditions, and a map. Readings come from public feeds, including NOAA SWPC, NASA DONKI, GFZ, and TEC.

route

Route risk

Score a drive, not only a pin. A simulate control shows the same route under a solar superstorm.

mail

Email alerts

A subscription is an email address and a region. Subscribers get one daily digest, plus an extra message when the storm rule trips.

newspaper

Monthly newsletter

On the first of the month the service publishes a short retrospective of the previous month, with charts, from archived open data. If a feed is missing, the page marks degraded mode and still scores from the readings it has.
